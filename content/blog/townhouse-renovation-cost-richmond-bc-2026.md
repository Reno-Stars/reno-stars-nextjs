<h2>What Drives Townhouse Renovation Costs in Richmond</h2>
<p>Three factors account for most of the cost difference between comparable townhouse renovations in Richmond:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Strata approval requirements:</strong> Most Richmond townhouse complexes require strata approval before any renovation that affects common property, structural elements, or exterior appearance. Approval timelines vary from 2 to 6 weeks depending on the complexity of the project and the strata council schedule. Reno Stars handles the approval application process for clients, including preparing the required documentation and coordinating with property managers.</li>
<li><strong>Plumbing in slab foundations:</strong> Many Richmond townhouse developments built in the 1980s and 1990s have concrete slab ground floors. Moving drains or water lines in a slab requires breaking concrete, which adds $1,500–$4,500 per fixture moved and extends the project timeline by 1–2 weeks.</li>
<li><strong>Electrical panel capacity:</strong> Older townhouse units may have 60-amp or 100-amp electrical services that require upgrading before major appliance installations. An electrical panel upgrade to 200 amps typically costs $2,500–$4,500 and requires a separate permit from the City of Richmond.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Richmond Townhouse Renovation Permit Requirements</h2>
<p>In the City of Richmond, townhouse renovations require building permits in the following situations:</p>
<ul>
<li>Any work that changes the structural configuration of the unit</li>
<li>Plumbing fixture relocation (moving toilets, showers, or vanities to different walls)</li>
<li>Electrical rewiring or panel upgrades</li>
<li>Any work that affects common property or shared walls with neighbouring units</li>
<li>Installation of hardwired appliances (ranges, dryers, electric vehicle chargers)</li>
</ul>
<p>Richmond's building permit fees for residential townhouse renovations typically range from $400 to $1,200 depending on the project value.
