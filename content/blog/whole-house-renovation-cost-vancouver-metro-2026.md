# How Much Does a Whole House Renovation Cost in Vancouver & Metro Vancouver (2026)

In Metro Vancouver, a whole house renovation in 2026 costs between $175 and $350 per square foot on average, depending on scope, finish level, and city. A full renovation of a 2,000 sq ft home typically runs $350,000 to $700,000, while a whole-home renovation covering all major systems and finishes on a 3,000 sq ft house can exceed $1,050,000 at the luxury end. ## What Drives the Cost of a Whole House Renovation in Vancouver?

Three factors determine where your project lands within that per-square-foot range. Reno Stars has completed whole-house renovations ranging from a $120,000 condo flooring refresh in Richmond to a $1.2 million luxury transformation in West Vancouver — the difference comes down to these variables:

### 1. Scope of Work

Renovating one floor versus an entire home is the single biggest cost driver. A kitchen-and-bathroom-only renovation on a 1,500 sq ft space may cost $150,000–$250,000, while gutting and rebuilding every room in a 3,000 sq ft house runs $750,000+. Reno Stars' recent projects include a two-bathroom whole-home renovation in Delta covering kitchen and all bathrooms for $380,000, and a full house renovation in Vancouver that included new kitchen, bathrooms, and new flooring throughout for $520,000.

### 2. Age of the Home

Pre-1980s Vancouver and Metro Vancouver homes often contain Poly-B piping — a type of plastic plumbing that becomes brittle and prone to leaks after 30–40 years. Replacing Poly-B pipes adds $8,000–$25,000 to a whole-house renovation budget, depending on the size of the home. Reno Stars offers Poly-B pipe replacement as a standalone service and includes full replumbing in comprehensive whole-home projects.

### 3. Finish Level

Standard finishes — laminate flooring, mid-range quartz countertops, builder-grade fixtures — keep a whole-home renovation at the lower end. Premium finishes — wide-plank hardwood, natural stone, custom millwork, designer plumbing fixtures — push costs to the luxury tier. Reno Stars' West Vancouver bathroom renovation with champagne gold European fixtures and custom features represents the luxury finish level, while the budget-friendly Burnaby bathroom renovation demonstrates what is achievable at a mid-range budget.

## Whole House Renovation vs. Renovation by Room: What Saves Money?

Doing a whole house at once rather than one room at a time can save 10–20% on overall cost. Why? Contractors mobilize once, materials are purchased in bulk, and temporary living costs are consolidated. Reno Stars' project data shows that the cost per square foot in a phased approach — kitchen one year, bathrooms the next — often exceeds the all-at-once cost when mobilization, permit fees, and repeated site setup are accounted for.
