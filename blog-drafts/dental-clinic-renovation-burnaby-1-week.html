How Do You Renovate a Dental Clinic in One Week?

You start before the clinic closes. The Reno Stars team pre-built all cabinetry off-site in the days before demo began. While the cabinetry was being fabricated, the crew mapped every trade — electrical rough-in, plumbing rough-in, framing, drywall, and millwork — so they could run in parallel rather than sequentially. A typical renovation stacks trades one after another: framing, then electrical, then cabinets. In a one-week dental clinic renovation, those trades overlapped daily, with checkpoint inspections each morning before work resumed.

What Did the Dental Clinic Renovation Include?

The project covered four distinct zones inside the Burnaby clinic:

  • Reception desk: custom curved reception counter replacing a dated linear desk
  • Staff kitchenette: mint-green laminate and brown counters replaced with full-wall white shaker cabinets, quartz countertops, and gold hardware
  • Exam rooms: floor-to-ceiling cabinetry with under-cabinet LED lighting added throughout
  • Admin workspace: integrated custom storage rebuilt for two workstations

What Was the Biggest Challenge?

The dentist had a fixed opening date. Every day the clinic remained closed cost the practice real revenue — not hypothetical opportunity cost, but measurable lost appointments. Beyond the timeline, the existing layout had inefficient zoning: poor traffic flow between sterilization, reception, exam rooms, and the staff break room, combined with outdated finishes that did not reflect the quality of care patients expected inside.

The solution was pre-fabrication off-site combined with a compressed parallel-trades schedule. Reno Stars used a single dedicated crew for the entire seven days rather than rotating trade contractors. The cabinet supplier templated quartz on day two so it could be installed on day six. Electrical and plumbing rough-ins were completed before cabinet install began. The clinic opened on its scheduled date.

Is a One-Week Dental Clinic Renovation Right for Your Practice?

A one-week schedule is achievable when three conditions are met: the scope fits within a single dedicated crew's capacity, off-site pre-fabrication can absorb lead time before demo, and the client accepts a premium for the compressed timeline. For dental practices in Burnaby, Coquitlam, or Vancouver where closing for a full month is not viable, a one-week rush renovation may be the only practical option — provided the contractor can commit a full crew and pre-fabricate before the clinic's final day of operation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a dental clinic renovation really be done in one week?

Yes, for cosmetic and millwork-heavy scopes. A one-week schedule requires pre-fabrication of cabinetry off-site before the clinic closes, a single dedicated crew rather than rotating trade contractors, and trades running in parallel rather than sequentially. Structural or equipment-heavy builds (new X-ray rooms, HVAC upgrades, dental chair installations) may still require 2–3 weeks regardless of scheduling.

How much does a dental clinic renovation cost in Burnaby in 2026?

A full cosmetic dental clinic renovation covering reception, exam rooms, staff areas, and admin workspace typically ranges from $85,000 to $200,000+ depending on square footage, cabinetry scope, and equipment rough-ins. Rush scheduling adds a premium. A one-week schedule for a mid-sized clinic in Metro Vancouver is generally 15–25% more expensive than a standard 4–6 week timeline.

What permits are required for a dental clinic renovation in Burnaby?

Interior cosmetic work — cabinetry, painting, flooring, and millwork — does not require a building permit in BC. Any work involving electrical rewiring, plumbing relocation, structural changes, or HVAC modifications requires a permit from the City of Burnaby Building Division (604-294-7130). A commercial occupancy change or change of use may also require a separate occupancy permit review.
