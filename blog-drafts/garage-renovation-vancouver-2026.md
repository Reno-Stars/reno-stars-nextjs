<h2>Do You Need a Permit for a Garage Renovation in Vancouver?</h2>

<p>Yes, and the permit type depends on the scope of work. Cosmetic work only (paint, flooring, garage door replacement) requires no building permit but an ESA permit for electrical panel upgrades. Insulation and drywall requires a building permit if walls are opened. Adding heat or plumbing requires a building permit plus separate mechanical and plumbing permits. Converting to a secondary suite (ADU) requires a full building permit demonstrating BC Building Code Part 9 compliance including separate egress, fire separation, and sound isolation. ALR properties in Richmond, Delta, and Surrey require additional ALC approval before the City issues a permit. Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond, and Surrey all require garage conversions to meet the same BC Building Code standards as an interior renovation. Reno Stars verifies all setback requirements before filing any permit application.</p>

<h2>Insulation, Heating, and Making a Garage Year-Round Usable</h2>

<p>The biggest single upgrade that turns a cold Vancouver garage into a livable space is insulation. A proper Vancouver garage insulation package for a year-round usable space includes: 2x6 wall framing with R-20 fiberglass or mineral wool batts; R-40 ceiling insulation; polyethylene vapour barrier with proper taping at all seams; spray foam rim joist insulation where the garage slab meets the framed walls; intumescent paint on the garage-side of the firewall if attached to the house. For heating, a ductless mini-split heat pump is the most cost-effective option. A 12,000 BTU unit heats and cools a standard 400 sq ft garage for approximately $2,800-$5,500 installed, and BC Hydro rebates of up to $5,000 are available for qualifying heat pump installations.</p>

<h2>Garage to ADU or Rental Suite Conversion in Metro Vancouver</h2>

<p>Converting a detached garage to a legal secondary suite (ADU) is one of the highest-ROI renovation decisions a Metro Vancouver homeowner can make, particularly in areas where rental vacancy rates remain below 2%. The BC provincial government introduced legislation in late 2023 removing municipal barriers to ADU construction, and Vancouver, Burnaby, and Richmond are now actively processing ADU permit applications that were previously backlogged for years. A garage-to-ADU conversion that produces a legal rental unit requires: separate entrance with stairs or ramp to grade; minimum ceiling height of 2.1 m (7 ft) over the required floor area; full bathroom with ventilation to exterior; kitchen or kitchenette with dedicated circuits; fire separation rating of 45 minutes between the suite and the garage; separate BC Hydro meter. The permit and inspection route for a garage ADU in Vancouver typically takes 8-14 weeks from application to occupancy permit. For a homeowner's home-based business use rather than a rental, an insulated, drywalled, and heated space with a bathroom rough-in achieves a functional workshop, studio, or home office at roughly half the cost of a full suite conversion.</p>
