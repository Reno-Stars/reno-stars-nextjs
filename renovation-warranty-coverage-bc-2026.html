What BC Law Requires of Renovation Contractors

British Columbia does not mandate a minimum warranty period for residential renovation work under the Home Protection Act. That means contractors can offer as little as a 30-day labour guarantee and still operate legally. The BC Consumer Protection Office recommends getting warranty terms in writing before signing any contract.

Most reputable contractors in Metro Vancouver offer at least one year on workmanship. Reno Stars provides 3 years — triple the minimum standard — on all interior renovation work including kitchen, bathroom, basement, and whole-home projects.

What $5 Million in CGL Insurance Covers

In addition to the workmanship warranty, Reno Stars carries $5 million in commercial general liability (CGL) insurance. This protects the homeowner, not the contractor:

  • Property damage caused by Reno Stars workers on-site — up to $5M per incident
  • Injury to third parties (delivery personnel, inspectors, neighbours) on the job site
  • Damage to existing structure or finishes caused by contractor negligence

Homeowners should always confirm a contractor carries CGL insurance before work begins. A contractor with only WSBC coverage (which protects workers, not your property) leaves homeowners exposed if something goes wrong.

What Renovation Warranties Typically Do NOT Cover

Understanding exclusions is as important as understanding coverage. Reno Stars' 3-year warranty does not cover:

  • Damage caused by homeowners (heavy impacts, improper use)
  • Normal wear and tear on finishes (scratched surfaces, worn grout from heavy use)
  • Issues caused by pre-existing structural defects not visible during the initial assessment
  • Changes made to the property by other contractors after Reno Stars' work was completed
  • Natural movement in older homes (foundation settling, wood moisture expansion)

The 3-year workmanship warranty is tied to the project address, not the homeowner. If you sell the property within the warranty period, the new owner inherits the remaining warranty coverage. Transfer is automatic — no registration required.

Does the warranty cover materials purchased separately by the homeowner?

The workmanship warranty covers installation quality only. What documentation do I need to file a warranty claim?

Keep your original project contract and any change order paperwork. The project address and original contract date are the primary references needed to confirm warranty eligibility. Photos of the original completed work are helpful but not required to initiate a claim.
