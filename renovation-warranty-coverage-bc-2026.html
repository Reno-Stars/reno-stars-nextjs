What BC Law Requires of Renovation Contractors

British Columbia does not mandate a minimum warranty period for residential renovation work under the Home Protection Act. That means contractors can offer as little as a 30-day labour guarantee and still operate legally. The BC Consumer Protection Office recommends getting warranty terms in writing before signing any contract.

What Renovation Warranties Typically Do NOT Cover

Understanding exclusions is as important as understanding coverage. Reno Stars' 3-year warranty does not cover damage caused by homeowners, normal wear and tear on finishes, issues caused by pre-existing structural defects not visible during the initial assessment, changes made by other contractors after Reno Stars' work was completed, or natural movement in older homes such as foundation settling or wood moisture expansion.

Does Reno Stars warranty cover work done by sub-trades they hired?

Yes. Reno Stars is the primary contractor and holds responsibility for all work performed on the project, including any sub-trade specialists they hire. The 3-year workmanship warranty covers the full project regardless of which tradespeople completed specific tasks.

Is the warranty transferable if I sell my home?

The 3-year workmanship warranty is tied to the project address, not the homeowner. If you sell the property within the warranty period, the new owner inherits the remaining warranty coverage. Transfer is automatic with no registration required.

What happens if I need emergency repairs during the warranty period?

Contact the Reno Stars office directly for any warranty-related issue. Emergency situations involving water damage or safety hazards are prioritised for next-day response. Non-urgent callback requests are typically scheduled within 5 business days.

Does the warranty cover materials purchased separately by the homeowner?

The workmanship warranty covers installation quality only. Materials supplied directly by the homeowner are covered for installation defects but not for product quality issues. Reno Stars recommends purchasing materials through their preferred suppliers to ensure full accountability.

What documentation do I need to file a warranty claim?

Keep your original project contract and any change order paperwork. The project address and original contract date are the primary references needed to confirm warranty eligibility. Photos of the original completed work are helpful but not required to initiate a claim.
