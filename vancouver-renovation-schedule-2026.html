How Does a Reno Stars Renovation Schedule Work?

Every Reno Stars project in Vancouver, Richmond, Surrey, and across BC starts with a site assessment and detailed scope document. That defines the schedule before a single material is ordered. Here is the typical sequence:

  • Week 1 — Demolition & Rough-In: Existing finishes are removed, framing is adjusted, and rough electrical, plumbing, and HVAC work is completed. For a bathroom, this includes waterproofing and subfloor preparation.
  • Week 2–3 — Structural & Rough Trades: Any structural changes, wall relocation, or additions to electrical and plumbing runs are finished. Inspections are booked at this stage.
  • Week 3–5 — Taping, Sanding & Priming: Drywall is taped, mudded, and sanded to a smooth finish. Primer is applied before any finish materials go up.
  • Week 4–6 — Finish Install: Tile, flooring, cabinetry, countertops, fixtures, and paint are installed. For a kitchen, this is when cabinets, the quartz island, and appliances go in.
  • Week 6–8 — Final Touches & Inspection: Caulking, grout sealing, trim installation, and final cleaning. A walkthrough with the homeowner confirms every item is complete.

Factors That Extend a Renovation Schedule in Vancouver

Every schedule below is based on a real completed project. Actual timelines on your project may be longer if:

  • Your building or stratas require a permit before work begins — permit timelines in Vancouver, North Vancouver, and West Vancouver can add 2–6 weeks before demo starts.
  • Custom cabinetry or imported fixtures are specified — custom cabinets from Vancouver suppliers typically require 3–5 weeks from measurement to delivery.
  • Structural changes involve load-bearing walls — an engineer assessment adds 1–2 weeks and may require a separate permit.
  • The home is occupied during the renovation — working around a family's schedule slows daily progress.
  • Specialty finishes like heated floors, custom stone, or imported tile require lead times of 2–4 weeks.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a kitchen renovation take in Vancouver?

Based on real completed Reno Stars projects, a standard kitchen renovation in Vancouver or Richmond takes 2–3 weeks for a cosmetic refresh and 4–6 weeks when custom cabinetry or layout changes are involved. Custom cabinets add 3–5 weeks of lead time before installation begins.

How long does a bathroom renovation take in BC?

A standard bathroom renovation in BC takes 3–4 weeks for a mid-range project and 6–8 weeks for a luxury bathroom with custom finishes, heated floors, or structural changes. A master bathroom renovation in Vancouver with custom features completed in 4–5 weeks at $14,000–$16,000.

Do I need a permit for a renovation in Vancouver?

Permits are required for any work that changes the structure, electrical or plumbing systems, or occupancy classification of a building in Vancouver, Richmond, Surrey, and most Metro Vancouver municipalities. A Reno Stars project manager confirms permit requirements during the scoping phase and handles the application process.

Can I live in my home during a renovation?

Yes, for most kitchen and bathroom renovations you can remain in the home. Dust barriers and negative air pressure management keep living areas separate from work zones. For a whole-house renovation or a multi-phase project, temporary relocation may be recommended.

What is the longest phase of a home renovation in Vancouver?

The finishing phase — tiling, cabinet installation, countertop templating and installation, and final paint — is typically the longest phase of a Vancouver renovation. After templating, quartz or granite countertops require 1–2 weeks for fabrication and delivery. Custom tile work also requires additional cure time between grout applications.
