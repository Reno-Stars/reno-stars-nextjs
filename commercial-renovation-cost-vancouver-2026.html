What Affects Commercial Renovation Cost in Vancouver?

  • Space type: A retail boutique renovation typically runs $15,000–$50,000. A medical or clinic fit-out can reach $300,000–$500,000+ depending on equipment and code requirements.
  • Permit requirements: Commercial spaces in Vancouver require building permits from the City of Vancouver or respective municipality. Medical and food-service spaces require additional inspections (Vancouver Coastal Health, WorkSafeBC).
  • Scope: A single-trade replacement (doors, flooring, paint) costs $5,000–$25,000. A multi-trade renovation involving plumbing, electrical, and structural work ranges $50,000–$500,000+.
  • Location: Retail on Vancouver's Main Street or Commercial Drive tends to be mid-range. Downtown Vancouver and Yaletown retail spaces command premium rents and premium build-out costs.

Dental and Medical Clinic Fit-Out Requirements

Medical and dental clinic fit-outs in Vancouver require compliance with multiple regulatory bodies. A dental clinic renovation must meet Vancouver Coastal Health infection control standards, WorkSafeBC occupational health requirements, and City of Vancouver building codes. These requirements add 15–25% to comparable commercial fit-out costs but are non-negotiable for occupied patient spaces.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a permit for a commercial renovation in Vancouver?

Yes. Any commercial renovation in Vancouver that involves structural changes, plumbing, electrical, or changes to occupancy type requires a building permit from the City of Vancouver or the respective municipality (Burnaby, Richmond, etc.). Reno Stars manages the full permit application process including submissions to Vancouver Coastal Health for food and medical spaces.

What is the average cost per square foot for a commercial renovation in Vancouver?

Based on Reno Stars completed projects: standard retail refresh runs $40–$80 per square foot. Mid-range commercial renovation (new flooring, millwork, paint, signage) runs $80–$150 per square foot. High-specification medical or clinic fit-outs run $200–$400+ per square foot in Vancouver.

How long does a commercial renovation take in Vancouver?

A small commercial project (single trade, under $25,000) takes 1–3 weeks. A mid-range commercial renovation ($25,000–$100,000) takes 1–3 months including permit lead times. A full-scale commercial build-out ($100,000+) takes 3–6 months in Vancouver, accounting for permitting, procurement, and construction.
