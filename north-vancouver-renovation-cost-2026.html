What Affects Your North Vancouver Renovation Cost?

  • Scope size: A powder room refresh costs $3,500–$8,000; a full luxury bathroom runs $40,000+.
  • Material selections: Standard porcelain tile vs. imported natural stone can add $5,000–$15,000 to a bathroom.
  • Structural or plumbing work: Moving drains or walls requires permits and engineering, adding $3,000–$10,000.
  • Age of home: North Vancouver has many post-war homes requiring updated electrical and plumbing to code.
  • Permit requirements: Bathroom renovations under $10,000 typically do not require a permit; structural or layout changes always do.
