What Drives Window Replacement Cost in Vancouver

Three factors move your quote the most in Metro Vancouver:

  • Frame material. Vinyl is the most affordable and lowest-maintenance for most Vancouver homes. Fiberglass outlasts vinyl in coastal humidity but costs 40–60% more. Aluminum is strong but transfers cold in winter — thermal-break frames reduce that penalty.
  • Installation complexity. Ground-floor windows are straightforward. Second-floor and above require scaffolding or swing-stage rigging, especially on Vancouver's narrow lot homes. Detached homes typically pay $300–$500 more per upper-floor window than attached townhouses.
  • Building envelope compliance. Vancouver's Building Bylaw requires air-leakage testing and vapour-barrier continuity for window replacements over a certain scope. Your contractor must pull a permit — budget $800–$1,600 for the combined building permit and energy compliance review.

Vancouver Window Permit Requirements (2026)

Under Vancouver's Building Bylaw, a full building permit is required when:

  • More than 25% of the window area in a wall is being replaced in a single project
  • The window is in a firewall or fire-separated assembly
  • The replacement changes the structural opening size

A simplified permit (no energy review required) covers single-window replacements under the 25% threshold. Permit fees in Vancouver range from $85 per window (simplified) to $2.20 per $1,000 of construction value for full permits.

Window Replacement vs Window Repair: When to Replace in Vancouver

Repair makes sense when the frame is intact and the seal failure is confined to one or two units. Full replacement is the better economics when:

  • Three or more windows in the same wall have failed seals (fog between panes)
  • The frames show wood rot or coastal corrosion (common in Vancouver under the 900mm annual rainfall)
  • The U-value of your current windows is above 2.8 — upgrading to triple-glazed ($2,400–$4,200 per window) cuts heating bills by 15–25% on North Vancouver and Tri-Cities elevations
  • You are preparing the home for sale — new windows recoup 72–78% of their cost at resale in Metro Vancouver (Renovation ROI Report, 2025)

Do I need a permit to replace windows in Vancouver?

Yes, if more than 25% of the window area in a wall is being replaced, or if the replacement changes the structural opening. Simplified permits ($85 per window) cover single replacements. Full building permits are required for whole-wall or multi-window projects.

What is the best window type for Vancouver's climate?

Fiberglass or vinyl double-glazed windows with a low U-value (below 1.4) perform best in Vancouver's wet winters and temperature swings. For West Vancouver and North Shore elevations above 100 metres, triple-glazed windows provide meaningful heating-cost savings despite their higher upfront cost.
