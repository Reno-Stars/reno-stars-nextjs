What Drives the Cost of a Vancouver Kitchen Renovation?

Four factors most commonly determine where a Vancouver kitchen renovation lands on the cost spectrum. First, cabinets represent 30 to 50 percent of the total budget — stock and semi-custom cabinets start around $8,000 to $15,000 for a standard kitchen while custom solid-wood cabinets run $20,000 to $40,000 or more depending on layout and finish. Second, countertops add $3,000 to $12,000 depending on material — laminate starts under $3,000, quartz ranges $5,000 to $10,000, and natural stone can exceed $12,000. Third, plumbing modifications — moving a sink, adding a pot filler, or rerouting supply lines — add $1,500 to $5,000 and may require a permit in Vancouver. How can I save money on a kitchen renovation in Vancouver?

Three strategies reduce Vancouver kitchen renovation costs without sacrificing quality. First, reface existing cabinet boxes and replace only the doors and drawer fronts — this saves $10,000 to $20,000 versus full cabinet replacement and takes 2 to 3 weeks. Second, choose quartz over natural stone for countertops — quartz costs $3,000 to $8,000 less than marble or granite and requires no sealing. Third, keep existing plumbing locations — moving drains or supply lines adds $1,500 to $5,000 and may require a permit.
